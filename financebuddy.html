FinanceBuddy — AI Finance App

A UX/UI concept for a personal finance management app with an integrated AI assistant, built entirely in Axure RP.

FinanceBuddy addresses the challenge of making personal finance accessible and intelligent. The AI system helps users organise expenses, identify smarter financial decisions, and receive personalised recommendations. The entire design focused on simplicity, clean navigation, and an intuitive user experience — from initial user flows through to high-fidelity interactive prototypes.
