FinanceBuddy is a UX/UI concept for a personal finance management app that stands out thanks to its integrated AI system. The AI helps users organize their expenses, identify smarter financial decisions, and receive personalized recommendations. The entire design was created in Axure RP, focusing on simplicity, clean navigation, and an intuitive user experience.
