3D Robot Model & Environment

A fully modelled, textured and rendered robot character — complete with a themed 3D world built from scratch in Blender.

The project involved designing a robot character with clean shapes, proper topology, and mechanical details that communicate its functionality. Beyond the character itself, a full themed 3D environment was built to place the robot in a cohesive world. The workflow covered modelling, UV unwrapping, texturing, lighting setup and final rendering — with an emphasis on a polished, visually cohesive result.
